Smart Engines introduced OCR for text recognition in 102 languages at a speed of 15 pages per second
Smart Engines scientists today announced a new generation of Smart Document Engine for instant and precise recognition of various documents and forms. The innovative system makes it possible to quickly scan the text documents and extract the necessary data using a mobile phone. Thus, Smart Engines has become the universal software supplier enabling businesses to fully automate and optimize document management workflows.
With the new mode, the Smart Document Engine user can instantly digitize any documents and hardcopies. Prior to that, for this purpose, they had to use a conventional flatbed scanner. Now all they have to do is put the correct document on the smartphone camera. The Smart Document Engine detects the document, recognises it, even if the paper is bent or damaged, and then displays the recognition results in a separate window.
“We can confidently state that scanners will be part of the past very soon. The scanner scans a printed sheet in 15-20 seconds, while our technology at the same time can both take a picture and recognize 3 sheets", commented Smart Engines CEO Vladimir Arlazarov, PhD in Computer Science.
Compared to the earlier version, Smart Document Engine gives the user new tools to monitor recognition results. The system displays the coordinates of all text objects in the image and other supporting information. For example, the degree of recognition confidence in a text string, a word, or each individual character. By using this data, the system user can further analyze the recognition result.
So, Smart Document Engine helps businesses not only to optimize the document entry processes, but also to solve other problems such as, for instance, finding documents in real time by keywords or creating electronic archives.
“We are proud to say that Smart Engines provides all the tools required to manage business documents. We can recognize ID documents, bank cards, barcodes, bills, payments and so on. The technology that allows you to recognize documents from a piece of paper is another of our great accomplishments, to which we have been pursuing for 10 years”, – adds Vladimir Arlazarov.
The system supports recognition of documents in 20 languages, including English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Polish, Swedish, Czech, Croatian, Serbian, Bulgarian, Kazakh Cyrillic and Latin, Generalized Latin, Turkish, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Arabic and Hebrew.
It supports a wide class of operating systems, including specialized operating systems for personal data processing: Cent OS, Ubuntu, Red Hat Enterprise Linux, SUSE Linux Enterprise Server, Arch Linux and other Linux distributions, MS Windows, macOS, iOS, Android.
